# Ezoic Rejection - Content Requirements Guide

Your site was rejected because it needs more valuable content and better user experience. Here's what you need to focus on before reapplying on **May 2, 2026**.

## Ezoic Content Requirements

### 1. **Minimum Content Volume**

- ✅ **At least 30-50 quality articles** (Ezoic prefers 50+)
- ✅ **Regular publishing schedule** (at least 2-3 posts per week)
- ✅ **Original, valuable content** (not duplicate or thin content)
- ✅ **Articles should be substantial** (500+ words minimum, preferably 1000+)

### 2. **Content Quality Standards**

- ✅ **Well-written articles** with proper grammar and spelling
- ✅ **Original content** (not copied from other sites)
- ✅ **Valuable information** that helps readers
- ✅ **Proper formatting** (headings, paragraphs, lists, images)
- ✅ **SEO-friendly** content with proper meta descriptions

### 3. **User Experience Requirements**

- ✅ **Fast page load times** (under 3 seconds)
- ✅ **Mobile-responsive design** (works well on phones/tablets)
- ✅ **Easy navigation** (clear menu, search functionality)
- ✅ **No broken links** or 404 errors
- ✅ **Professional appearance** (clean design, good typography)
- ✅ **Contact/About pages** (shows legitimacy)

### 4. **Traffic Requirements**

- ✅ **Consistent organic traffic** (at least 1,000+ monthly visitors)
- ✅ **Growing traffic trend** (not declining)
- ✅ **Traffic from multiple sources** (search engines, social media, etc.)
- ✅ **Low bounce rate** (users stay and read content)

## Action Plan Before Reapplying

### Phase 1: Content Creation (Next 2-3 Months)

1. **Publish Regularly**

   - Aim for 2-3 quality articles per week
   - Focus on your niche (AI, ML, Data Science, Programming)
   - Write comprehensive guides (1000+ words)
   - Create tutorials and how-to articles

2. **Improve Existing Content**

   - Expand short articles to 1000+ words
   - Add images, code examples, and diagrams
   - Fix any grammar or spelling errors
   - Update outdated information

3. **Content Ideas for Your Blog**
   - "Complete Guide to [Topic]"
   - "How to [Do Something] in Python"
   - "Best Practices for [Topic]"
   - "Common Mistakes in [Topic] and How to Avoid Them"
   - "Step-by-Step Tutorial: [Project]"
   - "Comparison: [Tool A] vs [Tool B]"

### Phase 2: User Experience Improvements

1. **Page Speed Optimization**

   - Optimize images (compress, use WebP format)
   - Enable caching
   - Minimize JavaScript and CSS
   - Use a CDN (Vercel already provides this)

2. **Mobile Optimization**

   - Test on real mobile devices
   - Ensure buttons are easily clickable
   - Check that text is readable without zooming
   - Test navigation menu on mobile

3. **Navigation & Structure**

   - Add a clear navigation menu
   - Create category/topic pages
   - Add a search function (you already have this)
   - Create an About page
   - Add a Contact page
   - Create a Privacy Policy page (you have this)
   - Create a Terms of Service page (you have this)

4. **Content Organization**
   - Organize articles by topics/categories
   - Add related articles sections
   - Create topic landing pages
   - Add breadcrumbs for navigation

### Phase 3: SEO & Traffic Growth

1. **SEO Optimization**

   - Add meta descriptions to all pages
   - Use proper heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
   - Add alt text to all images
   - Create internal links between related articles
   - Submit sitemap to Google Search Console

2. **Social Media Presence**

   - Share articles on Twitter/X, LinkedIn
   - Create a Facebook page
   - Share on relevant Reddit communities
   - Engage with your audience

3. **Email Newsletter**
   - Build an email list (you already have newsletter functionality)
   - Send weekly/monthly newsletters
   - Share new articles via email

### Phase 4: Before Reapplying (April 2026)

1. **Content Audit**

   - Ensure you have 50+ quality articles
   - All articles are 500+ words (preferably 1000+)
   - No duplicate or thin content
   - All images have alt text

2. **Technical Check**

   - Site loads in under 3 seconds
   - Mobile-friendly (test on Google Mobile-Friendly Test)
   - No broken links (use a broken link checker)
   - SSL certificate is active (Vercel provides this)
   - Site is accessible (no major errors)

3. **Traffic Verification**

   - Check Google Analytics for consistent traffic
   - Ensure 1000+ monthly visitors
   - Verify traffic is growing, not declining
   - Check bounce rate (aim for under 70%)

4. **Final Checklist**
   - [ ] 50+ quality articles published
   - [ ] Regular publishing schedule maintained
   - [ ] All pages load quickly (<3 seconds)
   - [ ] Mobile-responsive design verified
   - [ ] Navigation is clear and functional
   - [ ] About/Contact pages exist
   - [ ] Privacy Policy and Terms pages exist
   - [ ] No broken links
   - [ ] Consistent traffic (1000+ monthly visitors)
   - [ ] Low bounce rate
   - [ ] Professional appearance

## Alternative Ad Networks (While Waiting)

While you work on improving your site, you can try these alternatives:

### 1. **Google AdSense** (Easier Approval)

- Lower traffic requirements
- Faster approval process (usually 1-2 weeks)
- Good starting point for new sites
- You already have AdSense components in your codebase

### 2. **Media.net** (Yahoo/Bing Ads)

- Similar to AdSense
- Good for tech/content sites
- Lower traffic requirements than Ezoic

### 3. **PropellerAds** (Display Ads)

- Accepts smaller sites
- Multiple ad formats
- Good for testing monetization

### 4. **BuySellAds** (Direct Ad Sales)

- Sell ads directly to advertisers
- Higher rates but requires sales effort
- Good for niche sites
